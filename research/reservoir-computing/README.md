## Method and caveats

- Sources were found through web search on arXiv, Nature, APS, IOP, Wiley, OpenReview, and the IEEE RC Task Force. Searched 2026-10-01.
- Summaries are based on abstracts. The major reviews and the ParalESN/FRESCO papers were also read in part (conclusions, tables, open-problems sections).
- **Many 2026 items are preprints**, so check for later published versions. Paywalled papers (Nature Comms, Advanced Science, PRE, etc.) are linked but not downloaded.
- **No formal quantitative meta-analysis of RC exists.** [05](05-benchmarks-and-evidence.md) explains why and gives the closest available substitute.
